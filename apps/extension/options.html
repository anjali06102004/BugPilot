Connection, safety and experience preferences

Connection

Experience

Animations also respect the operating system's reduced-motion setting. The product is fully usable with sound and animation disabled.

Privacy and consent

This is only a preference signal for the backend. Screenshots and DOM excerpts stay on your BugPilot server unless the server operator enables page-text sharing. Passwords, tokens and payment fields are never collected by the extension.

Limitations

  • Authenticated areas require you to be signed in in this browser profile.
  • Cross-origin iframes and CAPTCHAs are not bypassed.
  • Enabling the extension never starts a scan; scope is always chosen explicitly.
  • The extension shows progress; the isolated Playwright worker performs deep exploration.
